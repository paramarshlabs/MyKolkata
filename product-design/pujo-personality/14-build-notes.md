What was built in the app for launch, where it lives, and where it departs from this bible and why. Written on 25 September 2026, when the build started.

## 2. How it works

1. **Landing** (`/pujo/personality`): static, public, no sign-in.
2. **Thirteen questions**, the **rapid round** (skippable), and the **tie-breaker** when the top two are within the margin. Progress is kept in `sessionStorage`, so a refresh carries on.
3. **The reading pause** (under two seconds), then **the reveal**: letterbox, Bengali name, sigil bloom, tagline. Reduced motion shows the final state at once.
4. **The result page:** lore on paper, why you got this (quoting the person's own answers), the Pujo DNA, streak, your people, what you'd say, "That's me?", tune it (status, diet, age), your Pujo (routes, pandals, plates, the playlist), sharing and invites, keep it, delete.
5. **Sharing:** the age question comes first. Then a card drawn on the phone (Story 1080 × 1920, Post 1080 × 1350), the native share sheet with the image file, save, copy link and WhatsApp. Under 18: the picture only, no links.
6. **Links:** `/pujo/you/{card}` shows a friend's card, with a compare if this phone already has a result; `/pujo/guess/{card}` is Guess my Pujo. Both have link previews; the guess preview never shows the answer.

Everything is scored on the phone. The typed engine matches the reference engine exactly (400 random quizzes in the tests).

## 3. Where the build departs from the bible

| Bible | Build | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Results saved in `pujo_results` with slugs and an API (spec §21, §22) | **No tables and no API at launch.** A result lives on the phone: `localStorage`, or `sessionStorage` only for under-18s. A shared card is carried in its link: 21 bytes, 28 characters (archetype, streak, band, badges, status and the fourteen DNA values). | Nothing to migrate or secure before Mahalaya, and nothing stored that nobody chose to share. The cost: links cannot be revoked or counted on the server. The app says so ("A link carries your archetype and your Pujo DNA, and nothing else"). The spec's tables are the path to revocable short links, squads and server recompute. |
| A display name on public cards | A first name can go **on the card image only**, never into a link. | Spec §23: no personal data in URLs. Links also end up in analytics and logs. |
| Server share images with pre-rendered Bengali (visual bible §6.4) | Link previews are built entirely from **pre-shaped outlines**: the Bengali, and the Latin too. Generated by `frontend/scripts/pujo/generate-paths.py` (HarfBuzz and fontTools), from the fonts in `public/fonts`. | Satori cannot shape Bengali. Clear Sans is a licensed Family Type web font, so it is not converted or copied to the server. Outlines of fixed lines are artwork, like exporting text to outlines. **Confirm the Family Type licence covers images of the typeface; if it doesn't, point the generator at an open-licence face.** |
| Sign in to save (should-have) | **Keep it on your account** writes the answers to the Supabase user's `user_metadata.pujo`, and brings them back on another device. Delete removes it. | No migration needed. Not tested against the live Supabase project from this environment. |
| Six preference questions | **Three:** status (goes on the card), diet (filters the plates) and age (privacy). | Movement, budget and side of the city feed personalised ranking, which isn't built yet. Asking for them now would be collecting without a use. |
| Personalised ranking | **Curated per archetype:** three routes, eight pandals, at least three plates tagged veg, egg or non-veg, things to try, and playlist anchors (YouTube Music search links). Every stop opens a Google Maps search. | The launch list the roadmap calls for. The page says "Picked by our editors… check before you go." |
| Question photographs (4:5) | **Typographic** question cards, and sigils on the landing and heroes. | The shoots haven't happened. The layouts leave room for photographs. |
| Offline after first load via a PWA | The quiz needs no network once loaded (scoring and copy are in the bundle). **No service worker yet**, so a cold start with no signal fails. | A service worker needs its own testing, and shouldn't be rushed. |

## 4. Not built yet

From the must-have list: the service worker, question photography, and the recommendation impression and open events (links are plain links, so the pages can stay static).

From the should-have list and later: squads, personalised ranking, pandal pages, tonight's route, the Bhog Map, playlist embeds, Pujo Wrapped, retake comparison ("Has your Pujo changed?"), the saved Pujo on the profile page, and the spec's tables and API.

## 5. Keeping it in step

- **The model:** edit and validate in [`model/`](model/), then copy the four JSON files to `frontend/lib/pujo-personality/model/`. `pujoPersonalityModel.test.mjs` fails until the copies match. It also checks that the port scores exactly like `scoring.mjs`.
- **The copy:** edit the character bibles and `07-pujo-match.md`, then run `node scripts/pujo/build-content.mjs` in `frontend/`. `pujoPersonality.test.mjs` fails until `content.json` matches.
- **The preview type:** after changing a name, a tagline or a fixed line in `og-lines.ts`, run `python3 scripts/pujo/generate-paths.py` in `frontend/`. The tests list any line without an outline.
- **Dates:** only in `frontend/lib/pujo.ts`, which now has Mahalaya on 10 October.
