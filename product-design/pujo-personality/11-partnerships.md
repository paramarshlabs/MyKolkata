# K. Partnerships

How brands, cafés, labels, events and Pujo committees can plug into the identity layer without it ever feeling like advertising.

Numbered sources refer to [`01-research.md`](01-research.md) §9.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **The identity is never for sale.** No logo on an identity card, a lore page or a sigil. A person's archetype is theirs.
2. **Labelled, every time.** Sponsored recommendations carry the word **Sponsored**, visible before anyone taps. Paid creator content follows ASCI's rules: an approved label such as #Ad or #Collaboration as the first element of the caption [78]. Generic tags like "#partner" are not enough.
3. **Relevant, or not shown.** A sponsored item must score at least as well, for that person, as the 60th percentile of the organic items it sits among. Relevance can't be bought.
4. **Archetype-level, never personal.** Partners target archetypes and zones. They never receive anything about an individual.
5. **Some data is never commercial.**
   - Nothing derived from Spotify or any streaming service (the Developer Terms forbid it even with consent) [8].
   - Nothing from the Roots dimension (ritual participation) or the Romance lens.
   - Nothing from anyone under 18 (DPDP Act s.9 forbids behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children) [77].
6. **One sponsored item per list**, capped at two impressions a day per partner per person.
7. **Brand safety.** No alcohol, tobacco, betting or real-money gaming, skin-lightening products, political parties, politicians or religious organisations as advertisers. Committees are editorial partners, never ad buyers.

## 2. The brief's example, rebuilt

> **You're a Pujo Romantic.** Recommended: a date-night route, an outfit, a dessert, a playlist, pandals, an evening event.

In the product, a Romantic's "Your Pujo" shows:

| Slot | Item | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Tonight | *Ei Path*, North: Kumartuli lanes, Bagbazar ghat at sunset, a rickshaw, College Square's lights | Organic |
| Pandals | College Square; a sabeki pujo at dusk; one theme pandal the Art Kids love | Organic |
| Plate | Daab sherbet at Paramount; mishti doi in a clay pot | Organic |
| Look | "Coordinated but deniable": laal-paar and one red thread, how to drape it | Organic (content) |
| Playlist | *Ei Path* | Organic (editorial) |
| Event | A dusk concert of Bengali love songs | Organic, with a ticket link that may earn a commission, disclosed |
| **Sponsored** | A handloom label's laal-paar edit, shown because it matches the look above | **Sponsored**, labelled, relevance-checked |

The sponsored item arrives inside the story the person is already reading, and it says what it is.

## 3. Formats

| Format | What it is | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Sponsored card** | One card in a recommendations list | Labelled; relevance floor; frequency cap; never in the first position |
| **Presented route** | "*Nabami Nishi*, presented by {partner}" | MyKolkata chooses every stop; a partner stop is included only if it passes the relevance floor on its own |
| **Archetype collaboration** | A co-created item for one archetype: a Pet Pujari plate at a restaurant, a Dhunuchi handloom edit, a Night Owl Walk with a mobility partner, an Art Kid print series with a paint brand | Editorially approved; credited; labelled as a collaboration |
| **Merch collaboration** | Archetype merch made with a label, an artist or artisans | Revenue share; artisans credited and paid |
| **Affiliate** | Organic events and bookings with a ticketing partner's affiliate link | "We may earn a commission" on the booking button |
| **Committee partnership** | Verified pandal pages, claimed and maintained by committees | Free; never a paid placement |
| **Insights** | Aggregated archetype and zone audience reports | Groups of at least 50; no individual data; no streaming-derived data |

## 5. What partners get, and how it's measured

- **Reach:** archetype and zone audiences (aggregated, at least 50 people per group).
- **Placement:** sponsored cards, presented routes, collaborations.
- **Measurement without tracking people:** impressions, saves and route starts in aggregate; redemptions through unique offer codes per partner, not per person.
- **Content:** co-created, editorially controlled, credited.

## 6. Sequencing

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Pujo 2026** | No paid partnerships in the launch build. At most one or two pilot collaborations (for example, a Pet Pujari dish and an Art Kid print series) to test labelling, relevance and reaction. Affiliate links on organic events are fine. |
| **Post-Pujo 2026** | Committee partnerships at scale. Merch collaborations. Audience insight reports once the numbers exist. |
| **Pujo 2027** | Packaged partnerships sold by archetype and category (one partner per category per archetype), presented routes, collaborations, the sponsored card. |

**Packaging (illustrative, for Pujo 2027):**

| Package | Contents |
|---|---|
| Archetype partner | Exclusive category partner for one archetype for the season: one collaboration, sponsored cards in that archetype's lists, an aggregated report |
| Route partner | "Presented by" on one archetype's routes for the five days |
| Event partner | A co-hosted archetype event (Night Owl Walk, Dawn Patrol, a draping workshop) |
| Merch partner | A limited archetype drop on revenue share |

## 7. Governance

- **Review:** every partnership is approved by the editorial lead, product and legal, against the principles in §1.
- **Relevance check:** automated (the relevance floor) and editorial (does it belong in this archetype's world?).
- **Complaints:** a "Why am I seeing this?" link on every sponsored item, explaining the archetype and zone match and offering "Hide this partner".
- **Takedown:** any partner item can be pulled within an hour during Pujo.
- **Annual review** of categories and brand-safety rules.

## 8. Anti-patterns

- Banners, interstitials, pre-rolls or pop-ups anywhere in the quiz, reveal or lore.
- A logo on the identity card.
- "Sponsored" hidden in grey microcopy, or placed after the tap.
- Partner items in the first position, or more than one per list.
- Targeting by anything from §1.5.
- Paying for a pandal's placement in routes.
