## 1. The loop

```
         Take the quiz
               │
               ▼
      Get an identity you'd claim ──────────────► "That's me" (the product works)
               │
               ▼
   A card that looks good in a Story ─────────► share (Instagram, WhatsApp)
               │                                    │
               │                           a friend sees it: "what am I?"
               ▼                                    │
   Compare / Squad / Guess my Pujo ◄────────────────┘
               │
               ▼
     The friend takes the quiz (their result first, always)
               │
               ▼
     A compare card neither of them can resist posting
               │
               └──────────────► back to the top
```

The loop has two engines. **Broadcast:** a card in a Story, seen by a few hundred people. **Direct:** a compare, squad or guess link sent to one friend or one group, which converts far better because it needs that person to exist.

## 2. The K-factor, honestly

Every number below is an assumption until the soft launch measures it.

| Step | Assumption | Source of the guess |
|---|---|---|
| Completers who share a card | 30% | Identity reveals are built to be shared; [A] |
| Views per Story share | 200 | A typical Gen Z account's Story reach; [A] |
| Views that tap the link sticker | 1.5% | Link-sticker taps are low; [A] |
| Landing to start | 60% | [A] |
| Start to finish | 70% | Target in the UX spec; [A] |
| **Broadcast K** | 0.3 × 200 × 0.015 × 0.6 × 0.7 ≈ **0.38** | |
| Compare invites per completer | 0.4, of which 35% finish | [A] |
| Squads per completer | 0.1, bringing 4 people, of whom half finish | [A] |
| Guesses per completer | 0.15, of which 40% finish | [A] |
| **Direct K** | 0.14 + 0.20 + 0.06 ≈ **0.40** | |
| **Total K** | about **0.78** | |

A K below 1 means the loop amplifies rather than sustains: every seeded person brings in about 1 / (1 − 0.78) ≈ 4.5 people in total. So launch still needs seeding (creators, campuses, press, partners), and the biggest levers are the ones with the most uncertainty: share rate and direct invites. Those are what the soft launch measures first.

## 4. Mechanics

### 4.1 The card and the link sticker

The default share is the Story card with the link sticker. The prefilled caption asks the question back: *Turns out I'm a Night Owl. Which Pujo are you?*

### 4.2 Add Yours chains

Creators start an Instagram Add Yours chain, *What's your Pujo?*, with their own card. Instagram shows every contributor on the sticker [26], so each post points to the next. Three creators per archetype, chosen so every archetype has someone admirable wearing it on day one. Paid creator posts carry an ASCI-approved disclosure as the first element of the caption (#Ad or #Collaboration) [78].

### 4.3 Compare

*"You and @rimi have completely different Pujos."* The spark pairs (Night Owl and Shiuli, Hunter and Addabaaz) produce the most shareable cards, because opposites make better stories than matches.

### 4.4 Guess my Pujo

Before comparing, the sender guesses the friend's archetype. The friend's reveal settles it. This is the "mildly offended" engine the brief wanted: people will take the quiz to prove a friend wrong. Only between people who have connected, and anyone can hide their result.

### 4.5 Squads

*"Your squad: three Night Owls, two Pet Pujaris, one Shiuli (who will be asleep)."* Percentages ("42% Night Owl") appear from five members. Roles make everyone in the group matter.

### 4.6 Tag a friend

Public archetype pages make "tag your Pet Pujari friend" posts possible without a quiz, which is how the archetypes spread as a vocabulary even to people who never take it.

### 4.7 City, para and campus pulse

"Kolkata is currently 31% Night Owl" is exactly the kind of line that travels, and exactly the kind of line that must be true.

| Level | Shown when | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| City | 2,000 or more results | Whole-number percentages, updated at most hourly, labelled "among people who took it" |
| Zone (North, South…) | 500 or more in the zone | Same |
| Para or campus | 100 or more signed-in results for that para or campus | Self-declared, one result per account, never from under-18s |
| Squad | 5 or more members | Only visible to the squad |

Never from Spotify data. No animated counters (DESIGN.md). Never shown for a segment small enough to identify individuals.

### 4.8 Archetype channels

Nine Instagram broadcast channels or WhatsApp communities, one per archetype, run by MyKolkata's editors. Each drops at its archetype's hour during Pujo: the Night Owl channel's route at 11 pm, the Shiuli's bonedi timings at 5 am. After Pujo they become the year-round community (the Night Owl channel on Christmas week, the Shiuli's on the Dover Lane conference).

### 4.9 Pujo Wrapped

On Dashami: the season in a Story, ending on *asche bochor abar hobe*. The second share moment, and the promise to come back.

## 6. Ethics of invites

- No cash or coupons for invites. The value is the comparison.
- Never gate the result, or any part of it, behind a share or an invite.
- No contact-list access, ever.
- No fake scarcity, no countdown pressure, no guilt copy.
- Invite links are rate-limited to prevent spam.
- Anyone can hide their card from comparisons and pulses.

## 10. Growth anti-goals

- Growth that creates crowds: MyKolkata never gathers people at a pandal on its own initiative during peak hours.
- Statistics without the data behind them.
- Minors in any pulse, leaderboard or comparison.
- A guess feature that becomes a way to mock people: only between connected friends, always hideable.
- Spam: invite limits, and no messages sent on anyone's behalf.
