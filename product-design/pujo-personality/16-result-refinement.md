## Audit and decision

The live result already has a strong My Kolkata signature: Bengali type leads, the archetype sigil owns the hero, the palette and single crimson action are recognisable. The local prototype made the photograph full-screen, dropped those controls, and repeated a slide-deck pattern of numbered scenes and equal cards. At phone width it looks like a poster, while the useful answer evidence and plan are pushed too far down.

Keep the original `ArchetypeHero`, its reveal, badges, share action, and story link. Do not redesign buttons or the underlying feature. This is a hierarchy correction below the hero, not a new theme.

## Research applied

- [Spotify Wrapped](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-12-03/2025-wrapped-user-experience/) and [Apple Music Replay](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/11/apple-music-launches-new-replay-experience-reveals-2022s-top-charts/) show the value of one personal insight per beat and a lightweight share moment. Borrow the legibility, not their saturated slideshow style.
- Pinterest mood research is useful for framing and pacing, not a source of publishable imagery. Use licensed documentary photography instead.
- My Kolkata's `DESIGN.md` and the Pujo Personality visual bible govern the result: dark identity, Pearl for reading, restrained motion, real city detail, and no AI-generated person passed off as documentary photography.

## The result sequence

1. **Identity:** the existing archetype hero structure and actions, now with an archetype-specific photographic cover and aligned tagline for all nine results.
2. **Recognition:** a compact, softly rounded three-image deck beside a short archetype-specific scene line. One vertical card is fully visible while the other two peek from behind its upper corners. Every six seconds while visible, a rear card rises to the front and the current card recedes into the stack. Previous/next and pause/play controls let people choose the pace; reduced-motion settings keep it still. Below it, three answer-backed observations read like marginal notes, not equal cards. At least one exact quiz-answer quote makes the result feel earned. Secondary streak stays in the hero.
3. **Listening:** the existing archetype's editorial Bengali/Indian music picks appear in a quiet, functional strip. If the user picked a quiz soundtrack, acknowledge it and lead the three listening picks with a matching track. Links open individual searches in YouTube Music; do not imply an embedded player or a created external playlist.
4. **Depth on request:** full lore and light/shadow in the Pearl reading band, initially collapsed. The hero's `Read your story` action opens it. The evidence, DNA, people, feedback, tuning, routes, pandals, food, share/compare/guess, save, retake and delete remain accessible.

The three photos are evocative scenes, never a claim to show the named route stops. They do not carry overlaid text. The licensed photo pool is sequenced differently for each personality. On mobile the carousel is inset and finite, not a viewport-filling poster. The result should be understood by the end of the recognition beat without reading seven paragraphs.

## Your Pujo plan hierarchy

The first route is the primary plan: a short editorial introduction beside a numbered, connected stop sequence, with a subtle night-to-dawn shift along the line. The line communicates order, not geography, distance, or travel duration. Each stop retains its Google Maps link and short reason. On phones the introduction sits above the same vertical sequence. Two alternate nights remain expandable beneath it.

Show three pandal suggestions first, prioritising places not already named in the primary route, then let readers reveal the remaining five. Mark any repeated place as already on the first route. A single licensed, archetype-matched Kolkata photograph sits beside the detours, explicitly captioned as atmosphere rather than one of the named places; never add a thumbnail to every stop. Food picks use an authored moment label (for Night Owl: 2 am, 3 am, first light), preserve diet filtering, and stack in reading order on phones. The closing ideas are a compact note rather than a third equal-card grid, with practical reminders separated. First-route labels reflect the archetype's actual time of day, not "night" for a morning route. This is progressive disclosure of the existing editorial recommendations, not an assertion that the route is navigationally verified.

Every personality has a fifth editorial photograph in the pandal chapter, separate from its cover and three scene-deck images. The accompanying pandal and food introductions are authored for the identity, not shared filler. Pandal Hunter and Art Kid get a second view of Pujo lights; Shiuli gets actual morning light; Pet Pujari's cover is the credited Kolkata street-food cart rather than an unrelated plated dish. These are still mood photographs, not documentation of the recommended stops.
